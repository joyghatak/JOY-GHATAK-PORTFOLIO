AI Diabetic Retinopathy Screening & Biomarker Quantification System
Jul 2026 – Sep 2026 • Deep Learning, Medical Imaging & Computer Vision
PyTorch • EfficientNet-B0 • UNet++ • Grad-CAM

End-to-end medical-AI clinical decision support system for automated Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) screening, referable DR triage, and sub-retinal biomarker quantification — integrating EfficientNet-B0 for 5-stage severity grading, UNet++ / Depthwise Separable UNet ensembles for multi-class lesion segmentation (Microaneurysms, Hemorrhages, Hard Exudates, Soft Exudates), ResNet-18 for anatomical landmark localization (Optic Disc & Fovea) and image quality assessment, calibrated via Temperature Scaling and explained using Grad-CAM visual saliency maps.

  • Stack: Python, PyTorch, Torchvision, Segmentation Models PyTorch (SMP), Streamlit, OpenCV, Plotly, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, ReportLab
  • Architecture: Multi-Stage Medical CV Pipeline (Dual IQA & Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization → Calibrated 5-Class EfficientNet-B0 Grading → 4-Class Lesion Segmentation UNets → Vessel & Landmark Localizer → Grad-CAM XAI → Automated Clinical PDF Reporting)
  • Performance: 85.7% Sensitivity · 96.5% Specificity for Referable DR (Quadratic Weighted Kappa κ = 0.862) · 40.5% ECE reduction (calibrated ECE: 0.032) · Latency: ~11.5ms GPU / ~35.7ms CPU (86.5 FPS)
  • Key Highlights: Explainable AI (Grad-CAM heatmaps), pixel-level microaneurysm & exudate segmentation, vessel density quantification, Optic Disc & Fovea spatial tracking, confidence calibration, and automated clinical diagnostic report generation.
🌐 Live App: dr-screening-system-joy.streamlit.app
NeuroScan AI — Explainable Brain MRI Tumour Segmentation & Uncertainty Quantification
Aug 2026 – Sep 2026 • Deep Learning, Neuro-Imaging & Explainable AI
PyTorch • Attention U-Net • EfficientNet-B0 • MC Dropout

Comprehensive deep learning and explainable AI framework for automated Brain MRI analysis — combining Otsu skull-stripping, an Attention U-Net for sub-region tumour segmentation & ROI extraction, an EfficientNet-B0 classifier for 4-class pathology grading (Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary tumour, and No Tumour), Monte Carlo (MC) Dropout (30 stochastic forward passes) and Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) for epistemic uncertainty quantification, and Grad-CAM visual saliency maps.

  • Stack: Python, PyTorch, Torchvision, Streamlit, OpenCV, NumPy, Matplotlib, ReportLab, SciPy
  • Architecture: Multi-Stage Neuro-Imaging Pipeline (Otsu Skull Stripping & Brain Crop → Attention U-Net Tumour Segmentation → Full-Image & ROI Classification → Fitted Uncertainty Ensemble [0.75 × MC Dropout + 0.25 × TTA] → Grad-CAM XAI → Automated Clinical PDF Report)
  • Performance: 98.79% Test Accuracy · 98.84% Macro F1 · Cohen’s κ = 0.9835 on BRISC2025 cohort (Segmentation: 0.8400 Test Dice · 0.7756 IoU)
  • Key Highlights: Sub-voxel tumour mask segmentation, epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty banding, Grad-CAM visual interpretability, 4-tier risk classification, fast inference mode, and downloadable clinical research PDF reports.
🌐 Live App: neuroscan-ai-joy.streamlit.app
